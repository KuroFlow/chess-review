Frequently asked
Is ChessReview really free?
Yes — 5 game reviews per day at depth 10 forever. No card, no signup. Upgrade when you want more.
How accurate is the analysis?
We use Stockfish with depth-calibrated accuracy scores designed to match professional tournament analysis tools.
Can I import games from Chess.com?
Yes — export any game as PGN from Chess.com or Lichess and paste it directly. One click, instant analysis.
What makes this different from Chess.com's review?
We show coaching messages per move, critical moments, phase analysis, and a White/Black move timeline. Built for learners, not just stats.
